Streaming Normal-to-Lombard Speech Conversion

A streaming framework for converting normal speech into Lombard speech under noisy environments, with low latency while preserving linguistic content and speaker identity.

01 About the Demo

This demo compares different Normal-to-Lombard speech conversion systems under different noise conditions. The speech signal is kept unchanged while the noise level is controlled by the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR).

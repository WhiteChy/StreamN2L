About

StreamN2L

StreamN2L is a low-latency streaming Normal-to-Lombard speech conversion framework designed to improve speech intelligibility in noisy communication environments.

The system converts normal speech into Lombard speech while preserving linguistic content and speaker identity, enabling dynamic speech enhancement for latency-sensitive applications.

All speech samples are energy-normalized and mixed with the same noise to enable a fair comparison of noise robustness.

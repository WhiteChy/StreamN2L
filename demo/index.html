01 About the Demo

StreamN2L performs normal-to-Lombard speech conversion for noisy communication scenarios. The following examples compare StreamN2L with several existing approaches under noise conditions.

All speech samples are energy-normalized and mixed with the same noise to enable a fair comparison of noise robustness.
